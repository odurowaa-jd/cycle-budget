##  The Vision
CycleBudget was born out of a real-world financial crisis. In my household, our primary breadwinner (my sister) struggled with "Information Asymmetry"—the gap between her monthly salary disbursement and the actual velocity of household expenditure. Despite a 2,000 GHS budget, the household often became stranded mid-month due to a lack of liquidity discipline.

I leveraged my **Economics background and Banking experience** to digitize the traditional bookkeeping method, creating a SaaS platform that treats household finance with the same rigor as institutional liquidity management.

## The Economic Reasoning
The core of this application is built on three economic pillars:

1.  **Management of Scarce Resources:** Implementing a "Staged-Disbursement" model that breaks a single monthly budget into 4 weekly "Liquidity Cycles." This prevents the *Present Bias*—the tendency to overspend early in the month.
2.  **Liquidity Constraints & Rollovers:** The system rewards fiscal discipline. If a user spends less than their weekly allotment, the surplus is "rolled over" to the next cycle, gamifying savings and increasing future purchasing power.
3.  **Variance Analysis:** By tracking "Amount Taken" vs. "Amount Returned," the app calculates the *Actual Cost of Living* in real-time, allowing for immediate budget adjustments rather than waiting for month-end reconciliation.

## The Impact
Since implementing this system:
*   **Overspending:** Reduced by a great amount by creating a hard "Weekly Wallet" wall.
*   **Transparency:** Shifted from physical ledger books to a cloud-synced dashboard accessible by both the provider and the spender.
*   **Empowerment:** My non-tech-savvy mother can now record transactions in seconds using a high-fidelity mobile interface.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
*   **Frontend:** Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons.
*   **Backend:** Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL (Supabase).
*   **Authentication:** Clerk (Multi-tenant secure sessions).
*   **Deployment:** Vercel.

## Key Features
*   **The Command Cockpit:** A unified view of the Monthly Vault (Unallocated Reserve) vs. Weekly Wallet (Assigned Funds).
*   **Dynamic Liquidity Movement:** "Top-Up" features that explicitly move money from the Vault to the Wallet, maintaining a strict double-entry balance.
*   **Longitudinal Analysis:** Collapsible historical archives that provide categorical "Heat Maps" of spending behavior over months.
*   **SaaS Ready:** Architecture supports individual households with custom currencies and dynamic budget limits.
